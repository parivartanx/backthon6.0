## Antimicrobial Class: Amphenicols
*Total Agents in Class: 1*

### Antimicrobial Agent: Chloramphenicol
* **Classification Group**: Amphenicols
* **Authorized Clinical Indications & Spectrum**: Broad-spectrum bacteriostatic antibiotic reserved for serious susceptible infections (such as typhoid fever, bacterial meningitis) when less toxic agents are contraindicated.
* **Adverse Reactions & Symptom Profile**: Nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, glossitis; serious risks include boxed warning for irreversible fatal aplastic anemia, dose-dependent bone marrow suppression, and gray baby syndrome in neonates.
* **Key Pharmacovigilance & Clinical Guardrails**:
- Hematologic Warning: Myelosuppression, neutropenia, or potential aplastic anemia.

* **Classification Group**: Beta-Lactams: Carbapenems
